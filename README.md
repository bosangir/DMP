# Data Management Plans: pre-class reading

Pre-class reading on Data Management Plans (DMPs) and the research data lifecycle for the
Research Data Management course in the Master of Epidemiology (MEPI), University of Antwerp, 2026–27.

**Read it online:** https://bosangir.github.io/DMP/

## Contents

- What a DMP is and why it is a living document
- The research data lifecycle (plan, collect, process, preserve, share, reuse)
- The DMP lifecycle: application DMP, initial full DMP, updates, final DMP
- Funder requirements (FWO, BOF, BELSPO, Horizon Europe)
- The six core sections of a DMP, with epidemiology examples
- FAIR, "as open as possible, as closed as necessary", and the GDPR
- Writing a DMP in DMPonline.be
- How UAntwerp, UHasselt and KU Leuven handle DMPs
- Checklist, self-test questions, glossary and full list of sources

## Main sources

University of Antwerp RDM pages, Hasselt University Library RDM pages, KU Leuven RDM guidance,
FWO, DMPonline.be (Belnet), the Digital Curation Centre, Science Europe and the FAIR principles
(Wilkinson et al., 2016). The full reference list is at the end of the page.
